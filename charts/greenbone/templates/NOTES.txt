Greenbone Community Edition has been installed.

To access the web interface, port-forward the nginx service:
kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "greenbone.fullname" . }}-nginx 443:443 9392:9392

Then access https://localhost for GSA and https://localhost:9392 for GSAD API.

For troubleshooting, check the logs of the pods:
kubectl logs -f deployment/{{ include "greenbone.fullname" . }}-gvmd
